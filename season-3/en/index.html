See the calculations

Each MT is calculated separately. Your reward share = your relevant score ÷ the sum of all relevant scores in that MT.

Global FLOCK

Usage share × [α + (1 − α) × √stake share] × gmFLOCK multiplier

Reserved FLOCK + MT rewards

Usage share × stake share × gmFLOCK multiplier

  • Usage share = your API spending ÷ total API spending on that MT.
  • Stake share = your staked MT ÷ the total stake in that MT.
  • Early α is expected to be around 0.9 and may change. The interactive chart uses example parameters.
  • Conceptually, the gmFLOCK multiplier is 1 + λ × G. G grows with diminishing returns on effective balances; the protocol sets λ. The illustrative curve is not the exact protocol formula.

A few useful answers

What are the three rewards?

Global FLOCK is split among MTs by API usage, then among participants by global score. Reserved FLOCK is extra FLOCK for one MT; MT rewards pay that deployment’s own token. The latter two pools typically release over about 180 days.

Why did my usage not count?

Check that the model ID in your API call matches the target MT. A model can have multiple deployments. Using another deployment does not increase your share in this MT.

Where can I see my rewards?

Check FOMO for actual rewards. FLOCK goes into one claimable balance; MT rewards are shown per token. An unchanged balance may mean the last round added no rewards.
